# Privacy Policy for FigPin Layer Separator Studio

**Effective Date**: August 3, 2026

**FigPin Layer Separator Studio** ("FigPin", "we", "us", or "our") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates and handles user data.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Privacy
FigPin operates **100% locally** on your computer. 
- **No Personal Data Collected**: FigPin does NOT collect, store, track, or transmit any personal information, usage metrics, device identifiers, or user analytics.
- **Local File Processing**: All images, graphics, posters, and generated Adobe Photoshop (`.psd`) files are processed entirely on your local machine using PyTorch CUDA and local model weights. No uploaded media or image content is ever transmitted to external servers.

---

### 2. Network Activity
FigPin accesses local network connections (`localhost:8000`) solely for communication between the desktop interface (WinUI 3) and the local AI processing backend server.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics
FigPin does NOT use third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, telemetry frameworks, or external analytics services.
